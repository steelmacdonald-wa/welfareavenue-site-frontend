Welfare Avenue

Join our team

Welfare Avenue is youth-powered. The people who plan the servings, run the kitchen, tell our story and raise the funds are volunteers like you.

See the open roles

Why join

Lead, learn and make it happen

Team roles take between 4 and 8 hours a week, flexible around your work and school schedule, mostly online, with a weekly team meeting. You get real responsibility, a team that has your back and work you can point to.

  • Real responsibility from your first week
  • Leadership and project experience for your CV
  • A reference letter and a record of your hours

Open roles

The roles we are recruiting for are in the form below. Not sure which fits? Send a general application.

How it works

  1. 01

    Apply

    Pick a role, attach your CV and tell us why Welfare Avenue.

  2. 02

    We get in touch

    Our HR team reads every application and calls or texts to set up a conversation.

  3. 03

    Meet the team

    A short interview with the people you would work with.

  4. 04

    Welcome aboard

    An offer, then onboarding with your team lead.
