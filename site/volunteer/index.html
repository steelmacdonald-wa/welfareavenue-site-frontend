Why volunteer

Join a youth-powered movement

Volunteering with Welfare Avenue is more than giving back: it is belonging to something bigger. No experience needed; our team leads show you the ropes on the day.

  • Build real connections in your community
  • Learn new skills in a supportive, welcoming team
  • See the difference you make at every serving

Volunteer in Montreal

Every second Sunday, our volunteers cook, drive and serve home-cooked meals in downtown Montreal. Pick a role, then the Sundays that work for you.

  • Serve

    • Montreal

    Set up, serve home-cooked meals with care and a smile, and pack up together. Servers arrive by noon.

    • Every second Sunday
    • About 2 hours
    What you’ll do
    • Meet the team at the serving spot downtown and help set up the tables.
    • Grab a pinny from the team leaders, wash your hands, put on gloves and serve.
    • Help pack up at the end. Afterwards we text you a two-minute survey, so we keep getting better.

    Bring: clothes for outside and the weather, and water for yourself. Clothes or toiletries to donate are welcome too; just let us know.

    Apply for this role

  • Cook

    • Montreal

    Cook one dish at home from our recipes, so every meal is balanced.

    • Every second Sunday
    • At home, ready by noon
    What you’ll do
    • Choose a dish from our recipes with your team leader before the Sunday.
    • If you live in one of our pickup areas, a driver collects it at your door and texts you about 10 minutes before. Otherwise, drop it off at the serving by noon, and stay to serve if you like.
    • Need containers? Tell us and we will bring some.

    Bring: your dish, packed, with a note of what is in it (allergens).

    Apply for this role

  • Drive

    • Montreal

    Bring the food and supplies to the serving, and everything back after. You need a car and a licence.

    • Every second Sunday
    • About 3½ hours
    What you’ll do
    • Main driver: meet the team leader at our storage unit and load up for the serving.
    • Pickup driver: follow a route to each cook’s door, and text each one about 10 minutes before you arrive.
    • Help bring everything back after the serving. Gas is reimbursed (up to $15).

    Bring: your licence, a charged phone and a clean trunk.

    Apply for this role

  • Take photos

    • Montreal

    Capture the day for our stories, with care for everyone’s privacy.

    • Every second Sunday
    • About 2 hours
    What you’ll do
    • Read our short content guide before the Sunday: what we like to capture and how.
    • Photograph the team, the food and the setup. No close-ups of the people we serve, and always ask before photographing anyone.
    • Send us everything you captured within 24 hours.

    Bring: your camera or phone, charged.

    Apply for this role

  • Community lead

    • Montreal

    Be the team’s link with the people we serve: welcome them, listen, and share what you heard.

    • Every second Sunday
    • About 2½ hours
    • After 2 servings
    What you’ll do
    • One spot per serving, for volunteers who have served with us at least twice. We confirm it with you before the Sunday.
    • Arrive about 15 minutes early and check in with the team leader.
    • Welcome people in the line and talk with them while they wait and eat: how they are doing, what they need, what they thought of the meal.
    • After the serving, run the short community debrief with the team leader, and fill in a short form the same day about who we served.

    Bring: your phone, charged.

    Apply for this role

    Collaborate with us

    Volunteer with your group

    Schools, clubs, companies and community groups can volunteer together. Tell us about your group and we will plan the day with you.

    • In Montreal: serve a Sunday meal with our team, 10 to 15 people at a time
    • In Toronto: cook with the team at our monthly community event
    • Anywhere: run a food or essentials drive, host a fundraiser or help with the Holiday Gift Basket Drive

    Please write at least four weeks ahead. We can provide letters for volunteer and community service hours.
