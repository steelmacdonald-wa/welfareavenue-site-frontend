What the drive is

A holiday tradition, three years in

The Holiday Gift Basket Drive is our biggest moment of the year. Donations come in through November, then our volunteers gather in December to wrap everything and hand-deliver it before the holidays.

Cozy socks, warm hats, toys and care packages go to guests at Montreal shelters and to young patients in hospital, a small reminder that someone was thinking of them.

Since 2024, we’ve run the drive with our partners at Scattered: more than 1,500 gifts that first year, then more than 3,000 last year.

What your gift provides

Gifts to the drive become warm clothes, hygiene kits, books and toys, wrapped by our volunteers and hand-delivered across Montreal this December.

Or give the amount you choose

Estimates from typical Canadian store prices for new items in 2026: about $5 for a pair of warm socks, $12 for a hygiene kit or for a child’s book or toy, and $40 to $50 for a full basket. Last year our volunteers packed 446 baskets for six Montreal shelters, from 46 to 100 each, and more for young patients in hospital. Every gift made on this page goes to the Holiday Gift Basket Drive.

How the season runs

  1. Mid to end of November

    Donations of gifts, essentials and funds come in.

  2. December

    Our gift-wrapping event, over one or two weekends. Dates are coming soon.

  3. Before the holidays

    Volunteers hand-deliver the gifts across Montreal.
