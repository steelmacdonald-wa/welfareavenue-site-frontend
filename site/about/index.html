Welfare Avenue

About us

A youth-powered non-profit fighting food insecurity and poverty through direct community action, delivering meals and essential support directly to people where they are.

Our mission

Solidarity, not charity

We lead with the person, not their circumstance. Everyone who comes to a serving is part of our community, and is welcomed that way.

Food is our starting point. From a first meal, we build trust and connect people with the support that can change what comes next: essentials, community services, housing and health resources, through the partners who provide them.

And we are youth-powered. Students and young professionals cook, drive, serve and lead, and grow into the next generation of community leaders along the way.

Four pillars

  • 14,000+meals served since April 2020
  • 250+servings since April 2020, in Montreal, Toronto and Ottawa
  • 4,500+holiday gifts hand-delivered in 2024 and 2025
  • 600+volunteers since 2020

Our best estimates from our records, April 2020 to September 2026, rounded down.

  • Food security mobilization

    Home-cooked meals, served every second Sunday in downtown Montreal by youth volunteers who cook, drive and serve. In Toronto, our team brings free food events to shelters and drop-in centres.

  • Dignified resource distribution

    Clothing, hygiene baskets and seasonal essentials, offered with care and choice. Our Holiday Gift Basket Drive hand-delivered more than 3,000 gifts in 2025.

  • Dismantling social isolation

    A meal is also a conversation. Many of our volunteers come back again and again, so the people we serve see familiar faces and a community that knows them.

  • Integrating the care ecosystem

    We build trust through food, then connect people with the services they need, alongside shelters and community organizations across the city.

Our story

Since 2020

  1. April 2020

    Welfare Avenue begins in Montreal. With a few friends and 100 sandwiches, our founder Steel MacDonald starts bringing food downtown during the first lockdown.

  2. Spring 2020

    Weekly servings begin, run from the trunk of a car with donated groceries and meals.

  3. March 2021

    Our first large public event, with Sportira, brings food and essentials to hundreds of people outside Berri-UQAM metro.

  4. June 2021

    We get our own tent, and setting it up becomes part of every downtown serving.

  5. October 2021

    Welfare Avenue incorporates as a Quebec non-profit.

  6. May 2022

    CBC News features our student volunteers, the start of a year of news coverage.

  7. October 2023

    The Canada Revenue Agency registers Welfare Avenue as a charity.

  8. September 2024

    Our first Toronto serving: 36 meals, with a new student chapter.

  9. December 2024

    Our biggest cook yet: with Adaptavist, 15 volunteers cook more than 250 homemade meals in under six hours for local shelters.

  10. December 2025

    Our second Holiday Gift Basket Drive, with Scattered and CEP, hand-delivers more than 3,000 gifts across Montreal.

  11. March 2026

    Our first Ottawa event, with about 20 University of Ottawa student volunteers.

  12. August 2026

    Our first Community Kitchen, with DESTA Black Community Network, and a new brand built on one idea: service is solidarity, not charity.
