Our activities

Every second Sunday we serve in downtown Montreal, and through the year we host fundraisers with our partners. Come to one, or bring one to life.

Sunday servings in downtown Montreal

Every second Sunday

Every second Sunday, our volunteers set up the tent downtown and serve home-cooked meals to everyone who comes.
