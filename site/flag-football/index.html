Flag Football Tournament

Our charity tournament is back with the Montreal Flag Football League. Registration isn’t open yet: it’s still early. Join the early list and you’ll be the first to know when it opens, with the date, the place and the team fee.

Join the early listWhat it’s for

What it’s for

Play for a cause

The Flag Football Tournament is one of our fundraisers. Teams spend a summer day on the field with the Montreal Flag Football League, and what we raise goes to Welfare Avenue’s programs.

That means free meals every second Sunday in downtown Montreal, essentials for people who need them, and a hand finding support.

The league has already signed on for next summer, so the tournament is coming back. The date is still being set.

Last summer, on August 8

  • 13teams took the field
  • 78players
  • $2,800raised for our programs

Thank you to Guru, DOSE and Lush for their support in kind, and to La Licorne de Feu for the food.

Questions about the tournament

Is registration open?

Not yet: it’s still early. Join the early list and we’ll tell you first when it opens.

When is the tournament?

In summer 2027. We’re setting the date with the Montreal Flag Football League, and everyone on the early list hears it first.

Does the early list cost anything?

No. It’s free and doesn’t commit you. The team fee will be announced when registration opens.

Do I need a full team?

No. Join the early list on your own and tell us you’re looking for a team.

Where does the money go?

To Welfare Avenue’s programs: free meals every second Sunday in downtown Montreal, essentials, and a hand finding support.

Can my company sponsor the day?

Yes. Last summer, Guru, DOSE and Lush supported us in kind and La Licorne de Feu brought the food. Join the early list and choose sponsor, or write to info@welfareavenue.com.
