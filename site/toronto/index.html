What we do in Toronto

Good food, cooked together

Welfare Avenue Toronto began in 2024/2025 as a student pilot project at the University of Toronto’s Mississauga Campus. In October 2025, Welfare Avenue relaunched in Toronto, hosting monthly free meal events where volunteers meet at a local community centre to cook and serve meals that are donated to homeless shelters, non-profits, and community organizations across the city.

  • Cook with a team, no experience needed
  • Prepare meals that go to shelters and community organizations
  • Meet students and young people who care about their city
