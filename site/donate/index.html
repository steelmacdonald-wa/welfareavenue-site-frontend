What your gift makes possible

  • $25One recipe from our cookbookThe groceries for a fruit salad for 8 to 10 people, or a tray of 16 no-bake oatmeal bars.
  • $75One volunteer cook’s batchA dish for 30 to 35 people, cooked at home by a volunteer, like our sweet potato and black bean salad.
  • $250A whole Sunday servingGroceries for about 50 plates downtown, each with a main, a side and fruit or dessert.
  • $20 a monthA recipe every monthAbout 100 portions over a year, served every second Sunday.

Estimates from our 2026 grocery orders and recipe book. Every gift supports all of our programs.
